## Claims

1. **Complete Tenant Scoping on Store Seam**:
   - Every evidence store method (`CreateBundle`, `CreateItem`, `StoreItemChunk`, `StoreItem`, `GetBundle`, `ListBundles`, `ReadItemData`, `FinalizeBundle`, `SetLegalHold`, `ExportBundleToTarGz`) takes `tenantID string` as its first parameter and verifies that the target bundle/item belongs to that tenant.
   - Unauthorized cross-tenant queries or mutations fail closed immediately with `ErrTenantMismatch`.
2. **Device Credential Authentication & Endpoint Binding**:
   - Updated `deviceOrLegacyMiddleware` to allow machine routes for response jobs and evidence submission while rejecting all other unapproved routes (`isAllowedDeviceRoute`).
   - Device credentials set `X-Role: tenant`, `X-Tenant-ID`, and `X-Device-Endpoint-ID`.
   - Handlers enforce that a device credential can only upload items to or finalize bundles created for its own registered `EndpointID`. Cross-endpoint attempts are rejected with `403 Forbidden ("device credential cannot upload to another endpoint's bundle")`.
3. **HTTP Route Authorization**:
   - `/api/v1/evidence/bundles`: GET (single/list) and POST enforce caller's authenticated tenant.
   - `/api/v1/evidence/items`: item registration and chunked/direct upload enforce both tenant and endpoint binding.
   - `/api/v1/evidence/finalize`: bundle completion and receipt creation enforce both tenant and endpoint binding.
   - `/api/v1/evidence/bundles/hold`: hold toggles enforce tenant boundaries and record the operator actor.
   - `/api/v1/evidence/export`: archive streaming enforces tenant boundaries and sanitizes download paths.
